Puppies learn fastest when everyone does the same thing. This page shows you how to set up your phone once, what to do with Wally day to day, and how to share what you notice.

One-time setup

  1. Accept the Wally Notes folder. Kelly has shared an iCloud Drive folder called Wally Notes with you. Open the invite link she sent (in Messages or Mail) and tap Open. It now appears in the Files app under iCloud Drive.

    Don't see it? Check that iCloud Drive is on: Settings, tap your name, iCloud, iCloud Drive.

  2. Put the hub on your home screen. Open the hub link in Safari (not another browser). Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add. Wally now has his own icon.
  3. Save a practice note. Open the hub, tap This week, scroll to Training notes. Type your name, write "Test note", and tap Save note to folder. If Safari asks, tap Download.

    The note goes to your Downloads folder. Your name is remembered for next time.

  4. Move it into Wally Notes. Open the Files app and go to Downloads. Tap the ⋯ button (top right), tap Select, tap your Wally note files, then tap the folder icon at the bottom and choose Wally Notes and Move.

    This is how notes reach Kelly. You don't need to do it every time: once a week, before Sunday evening, is perfect. Notes start with the date, e.g. 2026-10-20_…_your-name, so they're easy to spot.

Our words

Use these exact words, said once, in a normal friendly voice. If Wally doesn't respond, make it easier rather than repeating.

House rules right now

Please do

  • Reward Wally when he looks at you on his own
  • Reward calm: four paws on the floor, lying down, quietly watching
  • Keep training short, 1 to 5 minutes, and stop while he's having fun
  • Carry a few treats on walks
  • Let him rest. Tired puppies get wild.

Please don't

  • Let him greet dogs or people on leash this week. Cross the street or say "He's in training, we're saying hi from here!"
  • Scold, yell, jerk the leash or push him down
  • Hold him near something that scares or excites him "to get used to it"
  • Repeat a cue over and over

If Wally barks at a person or dog

Green: he notices but still takes a treat. Say "Yes!", treat by your leg, keep going.
Yellow: he stares, leans, goes stiff. Say "Let's go", turn and walk away cheerfully. Reward when he looks back at you.
Red: barking, lunging, won't eat. Calmly walk away. No scolding. Let him settle, then save a note with the colour Red.

What's worth a note

Save a note when

  • Something went really well (tag Win)
  • He got worked up: tag Yellow or Red, and say what set him off and roughly how far away
  • He tried somewhere new: a store, a friend's house, a busy park
  • You notice something new about what he loves or dislikes

No need to log

  • Every treat or every repetition
  • Ordinary, uneventful walks

Got a video? In Photos, tap Share, Save to Files, Wally Notes. Then mention it in your note, e.g. "video saved, 5:40 pm recall".

Every note helps. Move your notes into Wally Notes by Sunday evening. Kelly and Claude read them all and decide what Wally works on next.
